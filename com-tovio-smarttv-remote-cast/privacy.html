What we collect

The app does not collect or transmit personal data. Anything you create stays on your device.

Permissions the app asks for

Android permissions let the app do specific things on your device. Granting one does not mean the data leaves your device.

  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — to provide features that depend on your approximate location
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — to provide features that depend on your precise location
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to detect whether you are online
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to check your Wi-Fi connection
  • BLUETOOTH — to connect to nearby devices
  • BLUETOOTH_ADMIN — to manage connections to nearby devices
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT — to connect to nearby devices
  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN — to find nearby devices
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE — to discover devices on your local network
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to keep a task running while you use the app
  • INTERNET — to connect to the internet
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show you notifications
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — to open files you select
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO — to let you choose audio files from your device
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — to let you choose photos from your device
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO — to let you choose videos from your device
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED — to let you share only the photos you pick
  • RECORD_AUDIO — to let you record voice notes or use voice input
  • VIBRATE — to provide haptic feedback
