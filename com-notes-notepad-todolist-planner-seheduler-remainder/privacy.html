Permissions the app asks for

Android permissions let the app do specific things on your device. Granting one does not mean the data leaves your device.

  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID — to support Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION — to measure advertising performance without tracking you across apps
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — to support interest-based advertising via Android's Privacy Sandbox
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to detect whether you are online
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to keep a task running while you use the app
  • INTERNET — to connect to the internet
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show you notifications
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore your reminders after a restart
  • RECORD_AUDIO — to let you record voice notes or use voice input
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to deliver reminders at the exact time you set
  • USE_BIOMETRIC — to let you unlock the app with your fingerprint or face
  • WAKE_LOCK — to finish short background tasks reliably
  • BILLING — to process in-app purchases through Google Play
  • AD_ID — to show advertising and measure its performance

The app shows advertising. Ad providers may use your device's advertising identifier and limited information about how you use the app to select and measure ads.
