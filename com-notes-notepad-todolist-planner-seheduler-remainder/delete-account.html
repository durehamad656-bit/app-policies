About this page

This page explains how to request deletion of your Notely: Notepad & Tasks account and the data associated with it. Notely: Notepad & Tasks is published by AHS Studio's.

Android app package: com.notes.notepad.todolist.planner.seheduler.remainder

How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  • Open the app, go to Settings › Account, and choose Delete account. This removes your account immediately.
  • If you no longer have the app installed, email durehamad656@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject "Delete my Notely: Notepad & Tasks account". We will confirm and action it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your deletion request is processed, the following is permanently removed from our systems:

  • App activity — App interactions
  • App info and performance — Diagnostics
  • Device or other IDs — Device or other IDs
  • Financial info — Purchase history
  • Location — Approximate location
  • Personal info — Email address
  • Personal info — Name
  • Personal info — User IDs

What we may keep, and for how long

We may retain a minimal record of the deletion request itself, and any data we are legally required to keep — for example transaction records needed for tax or accounting purposes. Anything retained for a legal obligation is kept only for as long as that obligation requires, and is not used for any other purpose.

Backups are rotated on a 30-day cycle, so residual copies in backup storage are removed within 30 days of the deletion completing.

Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions before deleting, and note that deletion does not entitle you to a refund for past purchases.
